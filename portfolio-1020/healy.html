Visual Upgrade for Healy

设计前言

Healy是一家致力于女性日化用品的独立设计工作室，主要针对独立个性新潮的时尚女性研发更自然亲和的妆品。坚持手作和定制的个性方向，使得用户粘度极高，受众极其精准。创始人希望通过定制和手作的方式真正给到消费者安心靠谱的妆品，用标准化、定制化、流程化的打造每一款产品。品牌的核心理念为“自然、手作、美好”，迄今为止，Healy已经开拓了上海、北京、深圳等区域城市，忠实会员用户已有3000+。品牌创始人以及团队都是非常年轻的一群人，经过详细的沟通，以及视觉定义探讨后，我们了解到客户对于视觉的调性要求为个性简洁，但不呆板有足够丰富的延展度。如何切入“自然”这个核心点做出合适的视觉呈现是考量的一个重点

Design Preface

Healy is an independent design studio dedicated to women's daily cosmetics. It mainly develops more natural and friendly cosmetics for fashionable women with independent personalities. Adhere to the personalized direction of handmade and customized, so that the user viscosity is extremely high, the audience is extremely accurate. The founder hopes that the way of customization and handwork can truly give consumers peace of mind and reliable cosmetics, and create each product with standardization, customization and process. The core concept of the brand is "natural, handmade and beautiful". So far, Healy has developed regional cities such as Shanghai, Beijing and shenzhen, with more than 3,000 loyal members. The brand founder and the team are a very young group of people, after detailed communication and discussion of visual definition. We understand that the client's tonal requirements for visual simplicity, but not rigid enough to have rich extension. How to cut into the core of "nature" to make a proper visual presentation is a key point to be considered later
