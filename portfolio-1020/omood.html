O MOOD 巧克力品牌包装设计

O MOOD Chocolate — Brand & Packaging Design

O MOOD 是一家坚持以手作出品，原材料精心挑选的小众巧克力品牌。O MOOD 品牌为了保证出品的稳定性，阶段性限量出售，创始人始终坚持初心，坚持用好材料，用心做好产品。他认为一款好的巧克力能够影响消费者的情绪和心情，巧克力的制作是一种创意艺术而非简单的食品

O MOOD is a niche chocolate brand dedicated to handmade products and carefully selected ingredients. To ensure consistent quality, its chocolates are released in limited batches. The founder remains committed to good ingredients and thoughtful craftsmanship, believing that good chocolate can influence our emotions and moods, and that making chocolate is a creative art

Brand Logo

品牌标志我们采用尽可能简洁直接的方式呈现，以文字本身为主，结合了两个字母 OO 的变化，将文字标志结合符号化和情绪化的表达，让标志的记忆性和延展性得以提高，并且增加了些许趣味感，避免了纯文字标志的枯燥乏味

The logo takes a simple, direct typographic approach. Variations in the two O letters introduce symbolic and emotional expression, making the wordmark more memorable and adaptable while adding a sense of play

Brand Color

品牌色彩我们保留了原始的品牌色橙色，结合了部分的灰色和奶白色。既延续了品牌基础色彩资产，也让新的基础色在品牌以及包装上得到了新视觉呈现

The original brand orange is retained and combined with neutral gray and milky white. This palette carries the existing brand identity forward while creating a new visual expression across the branding and packaging

Brand Package

产品的包装设计的主视觉部分我们主要基于 LOGO 本身的创意延展，依旧是延续了品牌部分的设计方法，尽可能用简洁直接的文字结合标志本身来呈现表达。让包装形式追随功能本身，这也与品牌创始人的坚持初心不谋而合

The packaging extends the creative idea of the logo, continuing the same approach used for the brand identity. Simple, direct typography works with the mark to communicate the product. Form follows function, reflecting the founder’s commitment to the original vision
